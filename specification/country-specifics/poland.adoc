This section contains information about requirements and other data for invoicing in Poland.

==== Document types

PUF supports the following KSeF invoice types for Poland:

|===
|Invoice type |`cbc:InvoiceTypeCode` |Description

|`VAT`
|`380`
|Standard VAT invoice.

|`KOR`
|`384`
|Corrected invoice.

|`ZAL`
|`380`
|Advance payment invoice (`@name="ZAL"`).

|`ROZ`
|`380`
|Settlement invoice for advance payments (`@name="ROZ"`).

|`UPR`
|`380`
|Simplified invoice (`@name="UPR"`).

|`KOR_ZAL`
|`384`
|Correction of an advance payment invoice.

|`KOR_ROZ`
|`384`
|Correction of a settlement invoice.

|===

NOTE: The document type Credit Note (`381`) is not accepted by KSeF.

==== RestrictedInformation (document level)

For Poland, multiple FA(3)-specific values are carried with document-level `puf:RestrictedInformation` extensions using:

- `ext:ExtensionURI = urn:pagero:ExtensionComponent:1.0:PageroExtension:RestrictedInformation`

Each key-value pair is sent as one `puf:RestrictedInformation` entry. Use multiple entries when multiple keys or values are required.

|===
|Key |Description |Usage notes

|`placeOfIssue`
|Place of issue (`P_1M`).
|Text value, e.g. `Warszawa`.

|`checkoutMethod`
|Cash accounting scheme indicator (`P_16`).
|Boolean value `true` or `false`.

|`NrPartiiTowaru`
|Goods lot number (`WarunkiTransakcji/NrPartiiTowaru`).
|Can occur multiple times. Can alternatively be sent at line level via `cac:Item/cac:ItemInstance/cac:LotIdentification/cbc:LotNumberID` and will be collected from all lines. The document-level approach takes precedence.

|`WartoscZamowienia`
|Order value for advance invoice context (`Zamowienie/WartoscZamowienia`).
|Used for `ZAL`/`ROZ` scenarios.

|`P_15ZK`
|Total amount already paid in settled advance invoices (`P_15ZK`).
|Used for `KOR_ZAL` scenarios.

|`NrKSeFFaZaliczkowej`
|KSeF clearance ID of the referenced advance invoice.
|Used on `ROZ` and `KOR_ROZ`.

|`wz`
|Delivery note reference (`DaneFaKorygowanej/NrWZ`).
|Text reference value.

|`simplifiedTriangularProcedure`
|Simplified triangular procedure indicator (`P_TTP`).
|Boolean `true` or `false`. Required when the invoice is issued under the simplified procedure referred to in Art. 135 sec. 1 point 4 of the Act. If omitted, non-simplified procedure is assumed.

|`fp`
|Indicator if the invoice is referred to in Art. 109 sec. 3d of the Act.
|Boolean `true` or `false`.

|`marginScheme`
|VAT margin scheme indicator (`P_PMarzy`).
|Set to `true` when the invoice is issued under a VAT margin scheme (Art. 119 travel agents or Art. 120 second-hand goods/works of art). Must be accompanied by `marginSchemeType`.

|`marginSchemeType`
|VAT margin scheme type (`P_PMarzy` sub-type).
|Required when `marginScheme=true`. Valid values: `travelAgency` (Art. 119); `3` (Art. 120 sec. 4/5 — second-hand goods); `1` (Art. 120 — works of art); `2` (Art. 120 — collectors items and antiques).

|===

==== RestrictedInformation (line level)

Line-level keys are carried inside `puf:LineExtension` using:

- `ext:ExtensionURI = urn:pagero:ExtensionComponent:1.0:PageroExtension:LineExtension`

Each key-value pair is sent as one `puf:RestrictedInformation` entry nested inside `puf:LineExtension`. Multiple keys can be combined as separate entries on the same line.

|===
|Key |Description |Usage notes

|`universalUniqueLineNumber`
|Universally unique line identifier (`UU_ID`).
|Required by KSeF for certain invoice types.

|`GTU`
|GTU goods and services designation code.
|See https://pagero.github.io/puf-code-lists/#_gtu_codes_poland[GTU codes Poland^] for valid values.

|`KursWaluty`
|Per-line exchange rate to PLN.
|Use when different exchange rates apply to individual lines. For a single rate across all lines, use `cac:TaxExchangeRate` at document level instead.

|`originalLine`
|Flags the line as the before-state of a corrected line (`StanPrzed` for KOR/KOR_ZAL/KOR_ROZ, `StanPrzedZ` for KOR_ZAL/KOR_ROZ order lines).
|Set to `true` on the original line. See *Original line indicator* for all correction methods.

|===

*Example* +
_Line-level RestrictedInformation entries_

----
<Invoice>
    <!-- Code omitted for clarity -->

        <ext:UBLExtensions>

                <ext:ExtensionContent>
                    <puf:PageroExtension>
                        <puf:LineExtension>

                                <puf:Key>universalUniqueLineNumber</puf:Key>

==== Multi-party scenarios

Poland's KSeF supports additional parties beyond the standard seller and buyer. These are mapped using `puf:RestrictedInformationParty` at document level. The following keys are supported:

|===
|Key |FA(3) party |Description

|`factor`
|Podmiot3, Rola=1
|The factoring company to which the receivable has been assigned. Payment is routed to this party's bank account.

|`recipient`
|Podmiot3, Rola=2
|Internal unit, branch, or division of the purchaser that is the actual recipient but does not qualify as a purchaser under the Act. Can also be sent via `cac:Delivery/cac:DeliveryParty`.

|`primaryEntity`
|Podmiot3, Rola=3
|The original/primary entity in a multi-party transaction (e.g. the original contracting party when an additional buyer is present).

|`additionalBuyer`
|Podmiot3, Rola=4
|Additional buyer party. Used when more than one legal entity is purchasing (e.g. joint procurement). A `GEN:SharePercentage` registration data entry may be added to indicate the ownership share.

|`invoiceIssuer`
|Podmiot3, Rola=5
|Entity issuing the invoice on behalf of the taxpayer (self-billing agent).

|`payer`
|Podmiot3, Rola=6
|Entity making the payment on behalf of the purchaser.

|`JST-issuer`
|Podmiot3, Rola=7
|A local government unit (JST) that is the issuer of the invoice (e.g. a municipal authority issuing on behalf of a subordinate unit).

|`JST-recipient`
|Podmiot3, Rola=8
|A local government unit (JST) that is the actual recipient of the goods or services (e.g. a school under a municipal authority).

|`GV-issuer`
|Podmiot3, Rola=9
|A VAT group member that is the issuer.

|`GV-recipient`
|Podmiot3, Rola=10
|A VAT group member that is the recipient.

|`employee`
|Podmiot3, Rola=11
|An employee acting as a party in the transaction.

|`otherEntity`
|Podmiot3, RolaInna=1
|Any other party not covered by the roles above. A `partyDescription` restricted information value must be added to describe the role in free text.

|`correctedBuyerOrOtherPartyDetails`
|Podmiot2K
|The original buyer party as it appeared on the corrected invoice (KOR). Used when the buyer's data has been corrected.

|===

==== Tax category ID and tax type

For Poland, both the tax category identifier (`cbc:ID`) and the tax type (`cbc:Name`) are required on non-standard tax categories. Valid values for both can be found in https://pagero.github.io/puf-code-lists/#_tax_category_codes_poland[Tax category codes Poland^].

Use `cbc:Name` inside `cac:TaxCategory` (document level) and `cac:ClassifiedTaxCategory` (line level) to convey the Poland-specific tax type code.

==== Buyer identifiers

For non-EU buyers who do not have a Polish NIP or EU VAT number (e.g. export invoices), use `cac:PartyIdentification/cbc:ID` with a `schemeID` following the pattern `[CC]:TaxID`, where `[CC]` is the ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country code of the buyer (e.g. `US:TaxID`, `GB:TaxID`).

The supplier-assigned internal buyer identifier (`IDNabywcy`) can be sent using `schemeID="PL:IDNabywcy"` on the same element.

==== TSP commodity code

The Combined Nomenclature commodity classification code (`TSP`) is set at line level using `cac:CommodityClassification/cbc:ItemClassificationCode` with `listID="TSP"`. Used when goods must be classified under the EU Combined Nomenclature (e.g. for intra-Community supply). Repeatable for multiple classifications.

==== Original line indicator

There are three ways to correct a line in Poland, and PUF supports all three. This element applies to Method 1, where an indicator flags the line being corrected. When set, the indicator voids the original line.

Method 1 uses key `originalLine` (value `true`) in `puf:RestrictedInformation` at line level — see *RestrictedInformation (line level)* above.

For Methods 2 and 3, no indicator is required:

- Method 2: send the original line as a negative amount, then send the corrected line as it should appear. Similar to the first method, this method voids the original line, but by using a negative line instead. This method can be used when the receiver does not support the "original line" indicator in their receiving format, and it is more general and widely accepted.

- Method 3: send only the difference between the incorrect and corrected amounts.

Example (intended price 50 PLN, invoiced 100 PLN):

- Method 2: one line for -100 PLN and one line for 50 PLN.

- Method 3: one line for -50 PLN (bringing the total to 50 PLN).

==== Document reference in corrected invoice

To forward the details of the invoice being corrected, in a corrected invoice (KOR) the following fields can be used:

*Correction type*: Carried inside `puf:BillingReferenceExtension` as a `puf:RestrictedInformation` entry with key `correctionType`. Valid values: `1` — effective on the date of the original invoice; `2` — effective on the issue date of the correcting invoice; `3` — resulting in a different date.

*KSeF reference number*: The unique ID assigned to the original invoice by KSeF. Carried in `puf:ClearanceID` inside `puf:BillingReferenceExtension`.

*Reason for correction*: Textual note inside `puf:BillingReferenceExtension/cbc:Note`.

*Original invoice date*: `cac:BillingReference/cac:InvoiceDocumentReference/cbc:IssueDate`.

*Original invoice number*: `cac:BillingReference/cac:InvoiceDocumentReference/cbc:ID`.

*Example*
[source,xml]
----
<Invoice>
    <!-- Code omitted for clarity -->
    <cac:BillingReference>
        <cac:InvoiceDocumentReference>
            <ext:UBLExtensions>
                <ext:UBLExtension>
                    <ext:ExtensionURI>urn:pagero:ExtensionComponent:1.0:PageroExtension:BillingReferenceExtension</ext:ExtensionURI>
                    <ext:ExtensionContent>
                        <puf:PageroExtension>
                            <puf:BillingReferenceExtension>
                                <!-- KSeF reference number of the corrected invoice -->
                                <puf:ClearanceID>8375460033-20230829-F9E05396E2B1-FB</puf:ClearanceID>
                                <!-- Correction type -->
                                <puf:RestrictedInformation>
                                    <puf:Key>correctionType</puf:Key>
                                    <puf:Value>1</puf:Value>
                                </puf:RestrictedInformation>
                                <!-- Reason for correction -->
                                <cbc:Note>Textual note describing the reason for issuing a correction</cbc:Note>
                            </puf:BillingReferenceExtension>
                        </puf:PageroExtension>
                    </ext:ExtensionContent>
                </ext:UBLExtension>
            </ext:UBLExtensions>
            <!-- Original invoice number -->
            <cbc:ID>1234567</cbc:ID>
            <!-- Original invoice date -->
            <cbc:IssueDate>2023-08-29</cbc:IssueDate>
        </cac:InvoiceDocumentReference>
    </cac:BillingReference>
    <!-- Code omitted for clarity -->
</Invoice>
